Why ATS Scoring Matters

The Gatekeeper

Over 75% of resumes are rejected by Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) before a human ever sees them. These systems scan your resume for keyword density, strict formatting, and required experience based on the exact job description.

How Role-Pilot helps you bypass the filter:

Format Audit

We ensure your resume is machine-readable by identifying hidden tables, columns, or complex layouts that break parsers.

Keyword Gap Analysis

We compare your resume against the provided job description to highlight exactly which critical skills and buzzwords you are missing.

Actionable Feedback

We don't just give you a generic score. You get a prioritized checklist of exactly what to change to reach a 90%+ match rate.
