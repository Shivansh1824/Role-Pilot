def two_sum(nums, target):
    seen = {}
    for i, num in enumerate(nums):
        diff = target - num
        if diff in seen:
            return [seen[diff], i]
        seen[num] = i
    return []

Frequently Asked Questions

How do the AI mock interviews work?

Our TruGen AI avatars simulate real interview scenarios using advanced voice models. They analyze your verbal responses in real-time, evaluating your structure (using the STAR method), English fluency, tone, and technical accuracy.

Are my coding arena sessions private?

Yes. Your coding sessions and history are private to you. However, you can choose to make your best scores public on the Global Leaderboard so recruiters can view your verified capabilities.

How accurate is the ATS compatibility scoring?

Our ATS engine uses semantic analysis similar to enterprise tracking systems. It extracts keywords, assesses formatting, and compares your resume against live job descriptions to provide a highly accurate compatibility percentage.

What are the pricing details?

Role-Pilot is currently free for candidates during our initial rollout phase. Premium features, including unlimited AI sessions and recruiter introductions, will be available under a transparent subscription model soon.
