About Role-Pilot

Bridging the gap between engineering talent and modern hiring teams.

Our Mission

At Role-Pilot, our mission is to close the gap between exceptional tech candidates and recruiters by providing a deterministic, verifiable, and AI-powered preparation environment. We believe that securing a top-tier engineering role shouldn't rely on luck or expensive coaching, but on structured practice and transparent skill validation.

Core Platform Features

We've built a comprehensive suite of tools designed to simulate the exact conditions of modern technical interviews:

  • TruGen Voice Agent: Practice behavioral and technical screening rounds with our hyper-realistic voice AI. Get real-time feedback on your STAR method structure, delivery confidence, and communication clarity.
  • Technical Coding Arena: Solve real-world algorithmic and system design challenges in an IDE environment. Our AI instantly evaluates not just correctness, but your Big-O time and space complexity efficiency.
  • Global Tech Leaderboard: Move beyond static resumes. Earn XP for your successful coding submissions and mock interviews. Showcase your ranked performance directly to partnered recruiters looking for verified talent.

The 2026 Roadmap

We are continuously evolving Role-Pilot to remain the industry standard for career acceleration. Upcoming releases include:

  • AI Proctoring: Implementing non-intrusive, privacy-first session verification to ensure the integrity of the leaderboard rankings.
  • Verifiable Credentials: Generating cryptographically secure, on-chain proof of your coding proficiency and mock interview scores to share across professional networks.
  • Company Sandboxes: Exclusive integration environments where candidates can practice against the specific proprietary codebase structures of our hiring partners.
