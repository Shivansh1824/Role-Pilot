Privacy Policy

Your data security and privacy are our top priorities.

Introduction

Role-Pilot ("we", "our", "us") is committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. This Privacy Policy governs the manner in which we collect, use, maintain, and disclose information collected from users (each, a "User") of the Role-Pilot platform.

AI Voice & Video Processing Disclosure

During TruGen mock interviews, we utilize advanced voice and video processing APIs. Your raw audio and video feeds are processed in memory and are never stored persistently on our servers. The AI generates a transcript and an analytical profile (e.g., tone analysis, pacing, structural assessment) which is securely saved to your private dashboard. You maintain the right to delete these analytical records at any time.

ATS Resume Parsing and Data Extraction

When you upload a PDF resume to our ATS diagnostic tool, our system temporarily parses the text to evaluate keyword density, formatting structures, and compatibility against live job descriptions. The parsed metadata is stored to provide you with ongoing feedback, but the original raw PDF file is encrypted at rest and never shared with third parties without your explicit consent.

Data Retention and Recruiter Matchmaking

To power the Global Leaderboard, we retain your experience points (XP), anonymized technical scores, and coding submission history. If you opt-in to our recruiter matchmaking program, a limited profile including your verified scores and targeted technical stack will be visible to our partnered hiring managers. You can toggle this visibility off at any time from your account settings.

Telemetry and Analytics

We collect anonymized performance telemetry to improve the speed and reliability of our coding arena evaluations. This data includes average compilation times, error rate trends, and overall platform latency, but is strictly decoupled from your personally identifiable information.
