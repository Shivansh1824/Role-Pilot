About Role-Pilot

The enterprise multi-role AI voice interview platform simulating authentic committee loops.

Our Mission

At Role-Pilot, our mission is to eliminate the anxiety and unpredictability of high-stakes corporate interviews by giving candidates access to an ultra-realistic, multi-role AI voice committee. Real-world hiring loops are never conducted by a single person—they involve hiring managers, domain specialists, and cross-functional leaders. We replicate this exact panel dynamic with sub-160ms voice latency, natural turn-taking, and zero artificial delays.

Why Multi-Role Voice Committees?

Traditional mock interviews fall short because they use single-agent chatbots with robotic pauses and disconnected questions. Role-Pilot changes the paradigm through a collective 3-agent committee architecture:

  • Universal Chairperson (David): Anchors every panel, manages stage transitions, sets the agenda, evaluates leadership and cultural alignment, and moderates discussion.
  • Domain Specialists Across 4 Tracks: Specialized leaders for Tech & Systems Engineering (Alex & Mark), Product Leadership (Mark & Alex), Enterprise Sales & GTM (Marcus & Sean), and HR & People Operations (Sam & Ethan).
  • Conversational Turn Handoffs: Panelists explicitly converse with each other (e.g., "[David] Alex, let's explore their distributed systems architecture") and build upon the candidate's previous responses without repetition.

Voice Onboarding & Warm-Up with Nova

Entering a 3-agent committee can feel intense. That's why every candidate begins with Nova, our dedicated conversational AI voice prep copilot. In a low-pressure audio session, Nova:

  • Calibrates your microphone and ambient audio environment for crystal-clear WebRTC transmission.
  • Parses your resume and extracts a verified 300-word candidate fact-sheet to ground AI inquiries in your genuine experience.
  • Guides you through a friendly warm-up rehearsal to settle pre-interview nerves before the panel convenes.

Real-Time Voice Architecture

Role-Pilot is engineered from the ground up for high-fidelity, real-time auditory interaction:

  • Sub-160ms Latency: Powered by Agora Real-Time Communication (RTC) streaming audio.
  • Natural Speech Barge-In: Speak naturally and interrupt an AI interviewer mid-sentence. The system detects your voice via Deepgram STT and instantly yields the floor.
  • Evidence-Based Scorecards: Post-interview analysis maps spoken answers against the STAR methodology, citing verbatim timestamps, highlighting domain strengths, and diagnosing growth gaps.
