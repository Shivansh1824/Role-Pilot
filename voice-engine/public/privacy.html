Privacy Policy

Enterprise data security, ephemeral audio processing, and candidate privacy rights.

Introduction

Role-Pilot ("we", "our", "us") is dedicated to protecting your personal information, biometric voice data, and privacy rights. This Privacy Policy governs the collection, processing, and protection of information when you interact with our real-time AI voice interview platform, conversational onboarding agents, and evidence scorecards.

Real-Time Voice Streaming & Ephemeral Audio Processing

During voice sessions with Nova and our multi-role interview committees, audio is transmitted in real time using the Agora Software-Defined Real-Time Network (SD-RTN™):

  • Ephemeral Voice Pipelines: Spoken audio streams are converted to text in transient memory using Deepgram STT and answered via MiniMax TTS. Your raw microphone audio streams are never persistently recorded, stored on disk, or used to train public foundation models without your explicit consent.
  • Encryption in Transit: All voice packets are protected with industry-standard DTLS and SRTP encryption from your browser directly through Agora edge servers.

Conversational Transcripts & Evidence Scorecards

To deliver our core service—evaluating candidate responses and providing multi-agent committee interaction:

  • We generate textual transcripts during live sessions to coordinate turn-taking between AI panelists (e.g., David, Alex, Mark, Marcus, Sean, Sam, Ethan).
  • Transcripts are analyzed by our scoring engine to map answers against STAR behavioral competencies and domain depth criteria.
  • Generated scorecards, including cited quotes and growth feedback, are saved privately to your account dashboard and are visible only to you.

Resume Fact-Sheet Synthesis

When you provide your resume during onboarding or review your background with Nova, our system extracts a compact, 300-word candidate fact-sheet. This fact-sheet is utilized exclusively during that specific session to ground panelist questions in your verified background and prevent AI hallucinations. You can remove or re-upload your resume at any time.

Security & Sub-Processors

We work with enterprise infrastructure providers that maintain rigorous compliance certifications (SOC 2 Type II, ISO 27001, GDPR):

  • Agora: Real-time WebRTC audio transport and low-latency channel streaming.
  • Deepgram: Streaming speech-to-text transcription with continuous interim results.
  • Supabase: Encrypted database and user authentication with Row Level Security (RLS).

Your Privacy Rights & Data Deletion

You maintain full ownership of your interview data. Through your account settings, you have the right to:

  • Export your complete interview history and performance scorecards.
  • Permanently delete individual session transcripts or wipe your entire candidate profile with immediate effect.
  • Revoke browser microphone permissions at any time.
