Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our multi-role AI voice panel technology.

How does the multi-role AI voice panel work?

Unlike single-bot mock interviews, Role-Pilot puts you in front of a calibrated 3-interviewer committee. Universal Chairperson David leads the opening and culture rounds, then seamlessly hands the mic to domain specialists (Alex on architecture, Mark on product metrics, Marcus on pipeline, or Ethan on HR compliance) based on candidate answers.

Who is Nova and what is the voice onboarding warm-up?

Nova is our dedicated AI Voice Onboarding and Prep agent. She talks with you before entering the committee room, parses your resume achievements, verifies your seniority tier, tests your microphone latency, and calibrates the upcoming panel so you enter confident and focused.

Can I interrupt the AI panelists while they speak (barge-in)?

Yes. Powered by WebRTC voice streaming and a 160ms Voice Activity Detection (VAD) threshold, the moment you begin speaking, the active panelist yields immediately with zero robotic overlap or awkward delay.

What are the 4 specialized domain tracks available?

We provide four dedicated panels: Tech Engineering (David, Alex, Mark), Product Leadership (David, Mark, Alex), Enterprise Sales & GTM (David, Marcus, Sean), and HR & People Leadership (David, Sam, Ethan).

How does the Evidence Scorecard evaluate my performance?

At the conclusion of the interview, our system produces a timestamped evidence scorecard. Every piece of critique references the exact quote from your transcript (e.g. at [04:12]), linking rubric scores directly to what you actually said.
