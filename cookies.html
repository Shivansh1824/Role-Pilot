Cookie Policy

How we use cookies and local storage to deliver an ultra-fast voice simulation experience.

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you interact with a web platform. They allow web applications to maintain session continuity, remember interface preferences, and optimize network connections.

Essential Voice Session & Auth Cookies

Role-Pilot utilizes strictly necessary cookies to authenticate your account and preserve your active session state. This ensures that when transitioning from the Nova voice onboarding cockpit (`gear-up.html`) to the committee interview room (`/interview`), your authenticated credentials, channel authorizations, and panel parameters are seamlessly maintained without disconnection.

Theme and UI Preferences

We utilize client-side local storage to store your visual theme preference (Dark or Light mode). This ensures an instantaneous, flicker-free rendering of our glassmorphic interface whenever you return to the platform.

WebRTC & Audio Telemetry Cookies

To deliver sub-160ms voice latency, we monitor anonymous WebRTC connection metrics (such as round-trip time, jitter buffer status, and packet loss). These performance markers allow our edge network to dynamically select the closest Agora RTC gateway for your session.

Managing Your Preferences

You can manage or disable non-essential cookies via your browser settings. However, disabling essential authentication and session cookies may disrupt Agora RTC audio channel connections and interactive voice features.
