Cookie Policy

How we use cookies to provide a seamless platform experience.

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are stored on your device (computer, tablet, or mobile) when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently and provide information to the owners of the site.

Essential Session Tracking

Role-Pilot uses strictly necessary cookies to maintain your session state. This ensures that when you transition between the ATS Analyzer, the Coding Arena, and the TruGen Voice agent, your authentication status and assessment progress are preserved without requiring repeated logins. These cookies are critical for the platform's core functionality.

Theme and UI Preferences

We utilize local storage (which functions similarly to cookies) to remember your interface preferences, such as your chosen light or dark mode theme. This ensures a consistent, flicker-free visual experience across all platform pages upon your return.

Performance and Analytics Telemetry

With your consent, we deploy performance cookies to understand how users interact with the Role-Pilot platform. This helps us optimize load times, debug complex UI interactions, and improve the overall responsiveness of our web application. The data collected is aggregated and anonymized.

Managing Your Preferences

You have the right to decide whether to accept or reject non-essential cookies. You can exercise your cookie rights by setting your preferences in the cookie consent banner that appears upon your first visit, or by modifying your browser settings to decline cookies. Please note that disabling essential cookies may prevent you from utilizing our core AI features.
